Please note:

- Donations are **voluntary** and go to the author as a private person.
- Donations **unlock nothing**: they grant no features, no packs, no
  priority support and no license rights beyond the PolyForm Noncommercial
  License 1.0.0 that already covers the project (see `LICENSE`).
- Donations do not make your use commercial, and they do not permit
  commercial use by others. Commercial use by third parties still requires
  the author's written permission (see `COMMERCIAL.md`).
- This note is not legal or tax advice. Whether a donation is tax-deductible
  where you live is your own responsibility to check.
