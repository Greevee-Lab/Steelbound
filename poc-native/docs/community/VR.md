# Steelbound – VR

VR plays the mission from inside the cockpit with head tracking. Menus
and movies appear on a canvas in front of you.

## Requirements

- An active OpenXR runtime on your PC. Without one, **PLAY IN VR** is
  greyed out (**No VR runtime found (OpenXR)**).
- Meta Quest via Virtual Desktop: run the Virtual Desktop Streamer,
  connect the headset, and select VDXR as the OpenXR runtime in Virtual
  Desktop (its default).

## Launch in VR

- On the **Play** page, press **PLAY IN VR**. **Play on screen**
  (**LAUNCH GAME**) always starts without VR.
- **Desktop preview** (Display page, also on the ESC **VR** page) sets
  what the monitor shows while you play in VR: Side by side (both
  eyes), One eye (left), One eye (right), or Headset only (no preview).
  The preview follows your head.
- VR on/off applies after restart. Save it with **Save as default**
  (ESC menu) to keep it.

## Recenter

- **F8** centers the view (and the canvas) on your current head
  direction and position.
- The ESC **VR** page has the same function as **Recenter view**.
- The function can be freely bound: control **VR: recenter view** (for
  example to a gamepad or controller button) on the **Controls** page
  with **Assign …**.

## Canvas for menus

Outside the mission (logo, title, pilot menus, briefing movie, loading
screens), the monitor picture is shown on a canvas about 2.5 m in front
of you. Operate it as usual with keyboard or controller. When the
mission starts, the canvas switches off and you sit in the cockpit. F8
aligns the canvas with your viewing direction.

Seat height, world scale and eye distance can be adjusted on the ESC
**VR** page and act immediately.

## Known limitations

- Outside the area drawn by the game camera it is black. At far head
  turns you see the edge of the drawn picture; this is normal.
- During the mission, the ESC menu appears only in the monitor/preview
  picture, not in the headset.
- The canvas shows the monitor picture 1:1, including the 4:3 bars.
- VR is demanding. If the picture stutters, use the **Original (HD) –
  120 FPS** preset and check the load with **F2** on the monitor.
