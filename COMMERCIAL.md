## FAQ (draft, no legal advice)

**What counts as noncommercial?**

Personal, private use and modification without a commercial purpose, as
defined by the license text. The license text alone decides; this FAQ only
summarises it in plain words.

**My fork has a donation button / paywall / ads / paid features. Is that
commercial?**

Yes. Forks or distributions with their own payment models (donations to the
fork author, subscriptions, ads, paid downloads or paid features) count as
commercial use and require the author's written permission first. Voluntary
donations are only foreseen for the author (see `DONATE.md`).

**Can I sell texture or effect packs?**

Packs you made are your own responsibility, but selling them — or selling
access to them — counts as commercial use of the project and requires the
author's written permission first. Giving packs away for free to other
owners of the game is the intended, noncommercial way to share them.

**Can I stream the game or post videos?**

> **Proposal — decision rests with the author.** Streams and videos of you
> playing (for example Let's Plays, reviews or tutorials) are meant to be
> treated as fan activity and allowed, including the usual platform
> monetisation of the video itself, as long as you do not sell or paywall
> the software or packs. This paragraph is only a proposal until the author
> confirms it.
